# Sourdough Baguettes

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| Bread flour | 850 g | 100% |
| Water | 620 g | 73% |
| Ripe sourdough starter | 170 g | 20% |
| Fine sea salt | 17 g | 2% |

## Method

1. Mix all ingredients to a shaggy dough and rest for 30 minutes.
2. Give three coil folds over a 3.5 hour bulk ferment at 24 °C.
3. Divide the dough into 4 pieces of about 415 g each.
4. Shape each piece into a baton, proof for 45 minutes, then slash.
5. Bake at 250 °C with steam for 12 minutes, then 12 minutes dry.

## Allergen note

Contains wheat gluten.
