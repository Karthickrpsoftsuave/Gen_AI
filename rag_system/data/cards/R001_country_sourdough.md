# Country Sourdough Loaf (2 kg final dough)

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| Strong bread flour | 1000 g | 100% |
| Water (24 °C) | 720 g | 72% |
| Ripe sourdough starter | 200 g | 20% |
| Fine sea salt | 20 g | 2% |

## Method

1. Mix the flour and water and rest for a 45 minute autolyse.
2. Add the salt after the autolyse, then work in the starter and mix until smooth.
3. Bulk ferment for 4 hours at 24 °C, giving the dough three coil folds at 30 minute intervals.
4. Shape the dough, proof overnight in the fridge for 12 hours, then bake at 250 °C with the lid on for 20 minutes and lid off for 25 minutes.

## Allergen note

Contains wheat gluten.
