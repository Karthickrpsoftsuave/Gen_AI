# Fermented Jalapeño Hot Sauce

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| Jalapeño peppers | 800 g | 100% |
| Water | 200 g | 25% |
| Fine sea salt | 30 g | 3.75% |
| Garlic cloves | 40 g | 5% |

## Method

1. Blend the peppers, garlic and salt with the water and pour into a ferment jar.
2. Ferment for 5 days at 21 °C, burping the jar daily.
3. Blend and strain the mash, then measure the pH.
4. The pH must read below 3.8 before bottling; keep the bottled sauce refrigerated.

## Allergen note

None.
