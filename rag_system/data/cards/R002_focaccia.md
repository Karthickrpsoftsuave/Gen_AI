# No-Knead Fermented Focaccia

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| Bread flour | 500 g | 100% |
| Water | 400 g | 80% |
| Olive oil | 25 g | 5% |
| Fine sea salt | 7 g | 1.4% |

## Method

1. Dissolve the 7 g of fine sea salt in the water before adding the flour.
2. Mix to a sticky dough, then give four stretch-and-folds over 90 minutes.
3. Rest the dough in a well-oiled pan for 2 hours until it is puffy.
4. Dimple the surface, drizzle olive oil, and bake at 230 °C for 25 minutes.

## Allergen note

Contains wheat gluten.
