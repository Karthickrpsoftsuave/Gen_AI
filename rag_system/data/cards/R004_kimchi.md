# Baechu Kimchi

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| Napa cabbage | 1200 g | 100% |
| Coarse sea salt | 60 g | 5% |
| Water (for brine) | 900 g | 75% |
| Gochugaru | 45 g | 3.75% |
| Garlic | 30 g | 2.5% |
| Ginger | 15 g | 1.25% |
| Soy sauce | 20 g | 1.67% |

## Method

1. Quarter the cabbage and salt it for 2 hours, then rinse twice.
2. Make a paste from the gochugaru, garlic, ginger and soy sauce.
3. Massage the paste between every leaf and pack the jar tightly.
4. Ferment 3 days at room temperature, then move to the fridge.

## Allergen note

Contains soy.
