# 24-Hour Fermented Pizza Dough

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Weight | Baker's % |
|---|---|---|
| Caputo 00 flour | 600 g | 100% |
| Water | 420 g | 70% |
| Sourdough starter | 120 g | 20% |
| Fine sea salt | 18 g | 3% |

## Method

1. Mix the flour, water, starter and salt until no dry flour remains.
2. Give four folds during a 2 hour bulk ferment at 24 °C.
3. Ball the dough and cold ferment for 24 hours in the fridge.
4. Bring the dough to room temperature for 2 hours before stretching by hand.

## Allergen note

Contains wheat gluten.
